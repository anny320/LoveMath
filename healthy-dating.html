Important disclaimer

Love Math is an educational and self-reflection tool. It is not a psychological assessment, diagnostic instrument, professional counselling service or scientific predictor of relationship success. Scores and feedback are prompts for reflection, not definitive judgements about another person or a relationship.

Only assess what you have personally observed. A high score does not guarantee a healthy relationship; a low score or unanswered item does not automatically mean a relationship cannot develop positively. People and relationships are complex.

Always use your own sound judgement. Consider your lived experience, personal values, boundaries and circumstances. You remain responsible for your relationship decisions. Seek qualified support where appropriate. If you experience coercion, threats, intimidation, controlling behaviour or abuse, prioritise your safety and seek appropriate help; do not rely on a checklist score.
